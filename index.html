Scientific method

Mosteller BSA: √[(height cm × weight kg) / 3600]
Selected skin area: BSA × 10,000 × body-region fraction
Sunscreen: selected area × 2 mg/cm²

Scientific basis: 2 mg/cm² is the standard application density used in SPF testing. Adult regional surface-area fractions in this calculator are based primarily on the anatomical model used in published sunscreen-application research: face 3.5%, front neck 1.5%, back neck 1%, arms 14%, hands 3%, and legs including thighs/lower legs/instep 38.5%. Full body uses 100% of estimated BSA. For torso estimates, adult values from the same published regional model are used approximately.

Planning outputs: “Twice daily” is exactly 2 applications/day. “30 days” = per-application quantity × 2 × 30. The calculator reports mass in grams; it does not assume grams equal millilitres because product density varies.
